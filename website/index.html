Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up Swipy, macOS security, and spatial gestures.

How do I open Swipy on macOS? (Unsigned App / Gatekeeper Guide)

Swipy is 100% free and open-source software distributed directly via DMG without paid Apple Developer notarization certificates. When launching Swipy for the first time, macOS Gatekeeper may display a security alert stating that "Swipy cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software".

Instant Terminal Bypass
xattr -cr /Applications/Swipy.app

Or follow these simple steps in System Settings:

  1. 1
    Click the ? icon or Done / Cancel

    When the macOS security dialog appears, click the ? icon or Done / Cancel to dismiss the prompt.

  2. 2
    Open Privacy & Security in System Settings

    Click the  Apple menu in the top-left corner of your Mac, choose System Settings, and select Privacy & Security in the sidebar.

  3. 3
    Scroll down to the Security section

    Scroll down past FileVault to the Security section.

  4. 4
    Click Open Anyway

    Under the message stating "Swipy was blocked from use because it is not from an identified developer", click the Open Anyway button.

  5. 5
    Authenticate & Confirm

    Use Touch ID or enter your Mac administrator password, then click Open. macOS will permanently remember your authorization.

Is my camera video private and secure?

100% on-device and completely private. Swipy processes every camera frame in memory using Apple's native Vision framework on your Mac's Neural Engine. No video streams, photos, or 21-joint skeleton coordinates are ever saved to disk or transmitted over the network. Swipy works 100% offline without requiring any internet connection.

Zero Cloud Data 100% Offline Zero Telemetry In-Memory Vision Only
Which Macs and cameras are supported?

Swipy supports macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) and macOS 15.0 (Sequoia) or newer. It runs natively on all Apple Silicon chips (M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6) and Intel Macs with a camera.

Supported video inputs include built-in FaceTime HD cameras, external USB webcams (Logitech, Elgato, etc.), and wireless iPhone Continuity Camera.

How does Swipy affect battery life and CPU usage?

Swipy is architected in native Swift using Apple's Vision framework and CoreML Apple Neural Engine pipelines. CPU usage averages under 3.2% on Apple Silicon, with under 45 MB of resident memory. With sub-7ms tracking latency and 120Hz ProMotion display synchronization, Swipy runs silently in your menu bar with negligible battery impact.

Can I customize gestures or rebind shortcuts?

Yes! Every one of Swipy's 18 gestures can be individually toggled on or off, and remapped to any system action—such as Mission Control, Show Desktop, Previous/Next Space, Floating App Switcher, or Left/Right/Double Click—directly from the Gesture Library in Swipy Settings.
